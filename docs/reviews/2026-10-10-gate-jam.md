# The gate jam of 10 October 2026, and what the Pi could and could not hear

On 10 Oct 2026 a household D-Max (`172L66`) waited about nine minutes at the
gate while the Pi pulsed the relay four times for it. The driver has no fob
and was probably calling the GSM opener (RTU5024) on the same step-by-step
input. The gate ended up jammed and was later disconnected by hand. This page
records what the evidence shows, what it cannot show, why the controller
re-pulsed, and what was changed. Times are IST (UTC+1).

Evidence:

- **The Pi's journal and databases**, snapshot at 13:42. Earlier copies are in
  `~/Documents/dev/gate-evidence/2026-10-10/`.
- **The audio.** Every 300 s AAC segment from 9 Oct 21:00 to 10 Oct 11:35 was
  copied off the recorder's retention. Band levels were computed per 32 ms
  (the scanner's own bands, `gate_audio_detect.BANDS`) and aggregated to
  0.5 s.
- **A reference.** The 21 Sep test cycles from the R2 corpus (no vehicle, so no
  camera alarm), which is the only labelled motor audio available.
- **The camera's own configuration**, read only (`GetAudioAlarmV20`,
  `GetAudioCfg`, `GetLocalLink`).
- **D1:** events, notifications, left-open episodes.

Counts are small throughout. Anything resting on fewer than five cases says
so.

## Summary

- **The camera plays an alarm sound through its own speaker at every vehicle
  detection.** `GetAudioAlarmV20` has the audio alarm enabled for `AI_VEHICLE`
  all 168 hours of the week, at speaker volume 92.
  - The sound starts 0.2-1.9 s after each alarm and lasts 9-15 s (one 29.5 s
    when two alarms overlapped).
  - It drives the microphone to clipping (-5.7 dBFS, 1.2-8 kHz share 0.60).
  - All 12 vehicle alarms between 9 Oct 21:30 and 10 Oct 11:20 produced it,
    and it was the only loud high-band sound in those 14 hours.
- **The gate's motor is about 35 dB quieter than that.** On the 21 Sep
  reference the motor peaks at -25 to -46 dBFS with a 1.1-1.5 kHz line.
  - An idling diesel beside the camera (the D-Max, -47.5 dBFS, band +2-4 dB)
    sits at the motor's own band level.
  - So from 10:04 to 10:13 the Pi could not have heard the motor, whoever
    moved it: sirens covered about 75 s of the nine minutes, and the idling
    engine covered the rest.
- **The audio scanner (`gate_sound_scan`, yamnet-linear-v2) has been
  recording the siren and engines as motor runs.**
  - Every `gate_movements` row from 09:04 to 10:10 UTC is a siren or the
    idling D-Max.
  - The rows behind 9 Oct 21:42 ("open, close, reverse") and 22:22 include a
    siren and a departing or arriving car's engine. No motor-band run is
    visible in either.
  - The scanner missed four overnight sounds that do match the motor (below).
  - The left-open check (#202) reads those rows, so its "possibly open since
    22:22" was the siren.
- **The handoff's "a run 2.4 s before pulse 1: probably a GSM call" is the
  siren**, starting 0.5 s after the 10:04:11 alarm. **Which openings were GSM
  calls cannot be recovered from the audio.** There are no call logs.
- **Before 10:04 the gate was probably shut and latched.**
  - The recorder heard 98.5 % of 9 Oct 22:15 to 10 Oct 10:20; the only gap was
    the out-of-memory event, 07:43-07:54.
  - In that window it heard four sounds whose spectrum matches the reference
    motor: 23:24 (22 s), 00:25 (10 s), 00:26 (3 s) and 01:31:50-01:32:15
    (25.5 s).
  - The last ended with a clang at 01:32:14 (-22.9 dBFS, +35 dB over its
    background), the shape of the reference closing and latch (-22.9 dBFS,
    +37 dB). Nothing motor-like followed until 10:04.
  - This is one latch, so moderate confidence. Who moved the gate overnight is
    unknown: no camera alarm, no Pi pulse, no app command.
- **The Pi re-pulsed by design.**
  - Each camera alarm starts a new passage, and each frame gets its own claim
    key. The only thing between an authorised read and the relay was "has
    anything pulsed in the last 90 s".
  - Pulses 2 and 4 came from new alarms' sweep frames. Pulse 3 came from the
    camera's 4K FTP still, 92 s after pulse 2, 2.4 s after that alarm's own
    sweep frame was refused by the cooldown.
  - This breaks rule 1 ("never pulse a gate that is not provably shut and
    still") whenever a car waits longer than the cooldown.
- **No change shipped on 9-10 Oct contributed** (table below).

So the four Pi pulses are certain from the relay log. Whether each landed on
a shut, moving, open or stopped gate, and which runs were GSM calls, is not
recoverable. Whatever the order was, the controller sent pulses 2-4 into a
gate whose state it did not know.

## Every path that let the Pi pulse the same car again

These were quoted and checked against the journal on `38e970c`.

1. **New alarm, new passage.** `TriggerFrameCapture.run_forever`
   (`trigger_capture.py` ~1392) gives every event a fresh `SweepPassage`.
   `reolink_events` de-duplicates only the camera's `event_id`. The only
   throttle is the 5 s `min_interval_seconds`.
2. **The sweep ends on its own grant or on a cooldown refusal** (`note_result`,
   `PRESENCE_RETRY_REASONS`). So the waiting phase (#196) was not the
   re-pulse path: no pulse came from a waiting read, and every pulsing sweep
   ended `opened` within 1.6-4.8 s. What re-pulsed was the *next alarm's*
   sweep.
3. **The cooldown** (`actuation.py` `_cooldown_for`, `actuate`; `store.py`
   `_was_opened_since`) is time-only, any source, persisted. It does not look
   at plate or passage. A refused read is not queued; it ends the sweep. Its
   expiry is a clean slate for the next frame.
4. **`actuation_claims` is unique on the frame digest**, so twelve different
   claims for one car were all new.
5. **The camera's 4K FTP still is an independent grant path per alarm.**
   `superseded` only suppresses the sweep's own frames after the sweep opened.
   Pulse 3 is this path.
6. **The early trigger** was on but did not start any sweep before 10:36 (every
   would-trigger `skipped_unconfirmed`). It is a fourth path to the same
   coordinator.

**How the Pi learns about other openers: it does not, in time.**
- Its GPIO only drives its own relay coil, and the contacts are isolated.
- Fobs and the keypad go through the TOPENS receiver, and the GSM relay and
  exit wand switch the board's terminals directly. None of them shows on the
  Pi.
- Nothing on the decision path reads `gate_movements`, the latch, the
  left-open check or audio.
- The scanner runs on a 15-minute timer and skips the open segment. This
  morning its rows landed 7.7-18.6 min after the sound.

## The left-open alert and today's push

D1 has no `gate_left_open_episodes` rows at all, and no push went out for
this episode. Replaying `gate_left_open.evaluate` over the snapshot gives
these states:

- 9 Oct 21:42 burst: `likely`. Before #202 was deployed.
- 9 Oct 22:22 → 10 Oct 09:04: `possible` (two runs, even count). Shown, not
  pushed, by design.
- From 10:13 to 13:40: `possible`, never `likely`.

The states are built on rows that are sirens and engines. The two signals
that matter most for the alert, a closing heard and a latch heard, are
exactly what the siren masks when a car is present.

## The afternoon (13:35-15:20)

- **13:35 Audi `131D2696` "No plate read".** The car drove in through an open
  gate.
  - The camera's 13:35:52 still shows an empty drive, so the sweep's
    `departed` was right.
  - What failed was reading a 162-216 px plate in side light: 4 junk reads, no
    plate in either 4K still, the cloud unreachable. The arms were
    disconnected, so a grant would have moved nothing.
  - Focus: the daily refocus left sharpness 0.235 against the 0.18 threshold
    (n=2 days). The dashboard photo is a 1280×720 R2 copy and cannot show
    focus.
- **A separate, real defect behind the "read nothing" daytime sweeps of 8 Oct
  10:24 and 9 Oct 12:35** (handoff Task 3.1). `SceneBaseline.observe` adopts a
  keyframe 60 s after the last camera alarm, so a car waiting at a shut gate
  becomes "the idle drive" and its frames are skipped as empty.
  - Proven photographically on today's 10:05 and 10:07 samples, which show the
    D-Max parked and judged empty. Fewer than five cases.
  - The fix is to refuse a baseline frame in which the on-device detector finds
    a plate box. Not yet shipped.
- **About 14:50-15:20 the fob did not work** (the owner, at the gate).
  - The Pi's relay was open (GPIO19 low, read only).
  - No pulse or claim exists after 11:10:11.
  - The owner's power cycle rebooted the Pi at about 15:02; it shares that
    supply.
  - The recorder was not listening from 14:51, so the presses were not heard.
  - At 15:28:39 the owner, at the gate, pressed the app's hold-to-open
    (`remote_command`, event 3950). The actuators, detached from the leaves,
    ran a full cycle on the audio: about 20 s open, about 16 s hold, then the
    auto-close run from 15:29:18 to 15:29:35.
  - So the board and both actuators work from the step-by-step input. Only
    the fob path fails: the fob's battery, its pairing with the board's
    receiver (the board's CODE LED shows whether a press is received), or the
    receiver itself.
- **After the reboot the camera stream did not come back.**
  - `NetworkManager-wait-online` timed out (the powerline adapter came up
    late). `gate-media-gateway` failed five times with `bind:
    192.168.0.33:8189: cannot assign requested address`, and systemd stopped
    retrying. The transcoder died with it.
  - Restarted by hand at 15:20; audio resumed.
  - The camera's address had not changed: it is configured static at
    192.168.0.54.

## What was changed

- **The camera's vehicle audio alarm was switched off** (15:27, with the
  owner's OK; backup, verification and rollback are in
  [../reolink-rlc-811a.md](../reolink-rlc-811a.md#vehicle-audio-alarm-switched-off-2026-10-10)).
  The microphone now hears the gate at the moment a car arrives. The scanner
  rows recorded before 15:27 are still contaminated.
- **One car, one automatic pulse.** No further automatic pulse for a plate
  while that plate is still being seen. A pause switch,
  `GATE_AUTOMATIC_OPEN`, refuses every automatic grant when `off`. Both are
  enforced in the coordinator, so no path can route round them, and both can
  only withhold pulses. This is invariant 12, with guards that replay this
  morning (seven alarms, sweep frames and FTP stills, exactly one pulse).
- **The camera stream services retry after a late network** instead of giving
  up.
- This review, and the incident in `docs/gate-operator.md`.

## Not changed, and why

- **Withholding pulses on what the Pi hears.** Not possible as things stand.
  - The siren plays 0.2-1.9 s after every alarm, and the Pi pulses about 5 s
    after one, so a "motor heard in the last 90 s" rule would refuse every
    arrival.
  - An idling diesel hides the motor completely.
  - Prerequisites: the siren off (done 15:27), then a real-time detector
    validated on recorded passages, including engines idling beside the
    camera. It can only ever add caution.
- **The scanner and the left-open alert.** With the siren off, new rows are no
  longer sirens. Treat `gate_movements` rows from before 10 Oct 15:27 that
  start within 2 s of a camera alarm as sirens. Engines are still read as
  motor runs; that is a scanner fix to make.

## Recommendations

| # | Change | Why | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Move the relay from the step-by-step input to TOPENS `OPSW`** (open-only, where the exit wand is) | A pulse there can never stop, close or reverse the gate. It removes the jam mechanism whatever the software does. | Person at the gate |
| 2 | ~~Switch the camera's `AI_VEHICLE` audio alarm off~~ **Done 10 Oct 15:27** | It blinded the only gate sensor the Pi has at the moment that matters, and corrupted the scanner and the left-open alert | Done, with the owner's OK |
| 3 | **Give the Pi a real gate sensor.** In order of value: a magnetic or limit switch on each leaf (open / shut, whoever moved it); a sense of the TOPENS warning-lamp or motor output (moving); an opto-isolated input across the step-by-step and `OPSW` terminals (other openers' pulses) | Hearing is masked by sirens and engines; the latch was heard on under half of commanded cycles | Owner approves parts and wiring |
| 4 | Refuse an idle-baseline frame with a plate box in it | Waiting cars skipped as "empty" (8 Oct 10:24, 9 Oct 12:35, 10 Oct 10:05/10:07) | Code |
| 5 | Exclude the post-alarm window in `gate_sound_scan` and the left-open check | The siren is not the gate | Code, after #2 is decided |

For the hardware options, suggested parts are below; nothing is to be wired
without the owner's approval:

- **Leaf position:** two wired, normally-closed magnetic contacts (gate-grade,
  IP67, about 25 mm gap), one per leaf at the closed position. Wire them to
  two Pi GPIOs with pull-ups through a screened cable. "Shut" means both
  closed.
- **Motor or lamp sense:** a 24 V DC optocoupler module (for example PC817,
  4-channel) across the TOPENS warning-lamp terminals. That gives
  moving / not moving.
- **Input sense:** the same optocoupler, through a series resistor, across
  step-by-step–COM and `OPSW`–COM. This sees GSM and wand pulses. Check the
  board's input voltage first.
